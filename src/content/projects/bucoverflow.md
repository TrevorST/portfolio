BucOverflow extended **BucHelp** into a question-and-answer platform for anyone at ETSU who needed help. Students post a question, others reply, and the thread becomes a reference for the next person with the same problem.

Built with Django on PostgreSQL, with a CSS and JavaScript front end. Posts carry tags and votes, and the feed filters by rising, trending and topic.
