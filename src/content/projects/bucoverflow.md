BucOverflow extended **BucHelp** into a question-and-answer platform for the ETSU community's academic needs. Built from August 2022 to May 2023, it served **500+ unique users** and **10,000+ posts and discussions**.

- React front end, with Node.js and Express APIs behind it.
- Authentication, CRUD workflows and MongoDB persistence.
- Containerized continuous deployment with Docker and GitLab CI/CD to AWS EC2.
